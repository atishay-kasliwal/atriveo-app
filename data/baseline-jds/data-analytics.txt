Data Analyst

About the role
We are hiring a Data Analyst to turn product and business data into decisions. You will own
analyses, dashboards and metrics end to end, from data modeling and SQL through reporting
and recommendations to stakeholders.

What you will do
- Write complex SQL (joins, window functions, CTEs) against PostgreSQL, MySQL and cloud
  warehouses to answer business questions.
- Clean, transform and analyze data in Python with pandas and NumPy; automate recurring
  reports and ETL workflows.
- Build dashboards and KPIs in Tableau, Power BI or similar tools, and keep metrics accurate.
- Run A/B tests and statistical analyses (hypothesis testing, regression) and explain results.
- Define metrics with product, operations and finance partners and present findings clearly.
- Validate data quality, document sources, and monitor pipelines for issues.

Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, or a related field.
- Strong SQL and Python, Excel, data visualization and statistics.
- Experience communicating insights to non-technical stakeholders.
