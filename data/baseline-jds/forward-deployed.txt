Forward Deployed Engineer

About the role
We are hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer to work directly with customers and ship software that
solves their problems. You will own deployments end to end, from discovery and integration
through production rollout and support.

What you will do
- Build full-stack features and integrations with Python, Java, TypeScript and React, and REST APIs.
- Integrate customer data sources and systems; build data pipelines with SQL and ETL workflows.
- Deploy and operate services on AWS with Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD; monitor and debug in production.
- Build LLM and AI workflows (RAG, agents, prompt engineering) tailored to customer use cases.
- Work on site and remotely with customer engineering and business teams; turn feedback into product.
- Write clear documentation and run demos and training for customer users.

Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or a related field.
- Strong software engineering in Python or Java, SQL, cloud and system design.
- Excellent communication and customer-facing problem solving.
