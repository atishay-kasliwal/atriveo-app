Data Scientist

About the role
We are hiring a Data Scientist to build models and analyses that drive product decisions.
You will own problems end to end, from framing and data through modeling, evaluation and
deployment.

What you will do
- Build predictive and statistical models in Python with scikit-learn, PyTorch and TensorFlow,
  including classification, regression, clustering, time series and NLP.
- Analyze large datasets with SQL, pandas and NumPy; engineer features and validate data quality.
- Design experiments and A/B tests, apply statistical inference, and measure impact.
- Apply deep learning and LLMs (Hugging Face, retrieval and embeddings) where they help.
- Deploy models with data and ML engineering partners on AWS, and track experiments with MLflow.
- Communicate results with visualizations and clear write-ups to product and business leaders.

Qualifications
- Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, or a related field.
- Strong Python, R and SQL; advanced Excel; dashboards in Tableau or Power BI.
- Statistics, hypothesis testing and A/B testing; machine learning including XGBoost, random forests and model
  evaluation (precision, recall, F1).
- Experience taking models from research to production.
