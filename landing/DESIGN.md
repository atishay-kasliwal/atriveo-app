# Atriveo landing

Brief: a public introduction to Atriveo's job-matching workspace and Chrome companion, at https://apply.atriveo.com/landing/. Audience: people applying to jobs, and anyone the owner sends the link to. Job: explain the four connected stops (find and fit, evidence bank, tailored resume, companion and your own submit), show the real product, and send approved users to the workspace or sign-in. No open sign-up, pricing, customer metrics, testimonials or outcome claims. Every fact comes from README.md and docs/ (job-matching-workspace, unified-application-kit, bank-page, resume-builder).

Stack: static HTML and CSS generated by `scripts/build-landing.mjs` (templates in pages.mjs, all copy in content.mjs), plus one small module (`motion.js`: GSAP core + Lenis, about 33 KB gzipped, loaded as a deferred module). The public page never loads the private React console or calls its APIs. It is complete without JavaScript.

## Concept

Three territories, scored originality / fit / feasibility / portability (lower portability is better):
- Process, the application thread: one blue thread carries a selected job through evidence, resume and the companion. Anchor: the exact viewed PDF is pinned to the job all the way to the employer's form. 4/5/5/2. Chosen.
- Material, the proof sheet: an annotated application exposing the evidence behind each claim. 4/5/4/3.
- Formal, next brackets: bracket geometry around each move. 3/4/5/4.

```
Idea:        One job, one resume, carried through to a submit you make yourself.
Metaphor:    A blue thread through a dark application folio.
Signature:   The hero route: four stops on one drawn line; choosing a stop carries the line and its
             marker there (one driver: route.progress). In the story, the same thread runs down the
             left gutter and fills with scroll; each stage's node lights as the thread reaches it.
Emotion:     Clear and in control: nothing happens behind your back.
Motion verb: Carry. Lines draw forward, markers follow, fills sweep left to right (on dark, the button fill sweeps to off-white). No loader, no GL,
             no cursor replacement.
Screenshot:  "One job. One resume. Carried through." beside the blue route ending on "Submit".
```

## System

- Palette (single dark look, `color-scheme: dark`, `theme-color` #0b0f17; every colour is a token on :root): `--paper` #0b0f17 ground, a near-black with a blue bias, never pure black; `--raise` #121927 for chips and menus; `--ink` #e8ecf4 off-white text (about 16:1); `--muted` #9ba7ba (about 7:1); `--line` #273146 and `--shot-line` #34425f hairlines; `--blue` #6e98ff the thread, stage labels and accent text, brightened so it reads on dark (about 6.7:1); `--action` #2455e8 the filled-button and closing-field blue, which keeps white text at 6:1; `--field` #141d33 and `--track` #2c3a5c for panels and the undrawn thread. The blue closing field is the one bold place. No gradients. Captures are the dark console, so they get a 1px `--shot-line` border plus a deep shadow and a faint white ring to separate from the page.
- Type: DM Sans variable 400 to 700, self-hosted as one 36 KB Latin WOFF2 (OFL, see assets/fonts/OFL.txt). Display 60 to 112 px at -0.066em, leading 0.96; h2 40 to 76 px; body 17 to 18 px at 1.55 to 1.6; labels 12 to 14 px.
- Grid: 12 columns inside a 1280 px max wrap, gutter 64 px desktop, 16 px at phone width. Story stages sit right of a 96 px thread gutter (34 px on phones).
- Shape: 12 px panels, 999 px chips and buttons.
- Motion: one GSAP ticker drives Lenis, the route marker and the thread. Ease cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1) in CSS, power3.inOut for the route (0.9 s). Buttons sweep an off-white fill left to right; nav links draw an underline; arrows step up and right.
- Idle: the route marker breathes a short way back along the drawn line while the hero is on screen; it pauses off screen and under reduced motion.
- Reduced motion: native scroll, no idle, route stops still switch instantly, the thread shows fully drawn with every node lit. Without JS the route shows fully drawn at Submit.

```
  02 Evidence: thread | [text, 5 cols]          [evidence ledger: Strong .. Missing, 6 cols]
  03 Resume:   thread | [rules list, 4 cols]    [text, 7 cols]           (mirrored)
  04 Submit:   thread | [text + "Submit is always your click."]  [companion capture, 4 cols]
FAQ:      [Before you sign in.]   [native disclosures]
Close:    blue field: [Pick up the thread.] [Open the workspace] [Sign in] + thread ending in a dot
Footer:   wordmark, one line, footer nav, copyright
```

Captures are the real product UI with illustrative data only (Example team, Alex Morgan, example.test). They contain no private applicant information and are labeled as such on the page.

Genericness pass: no logo wall, numbers, testimonials, pricing tiers, gradient blobs, fake UI or equal feature cards. The ledger and the resume rules are typographic figures of real product rules, not imitation screenshots.

## Serving

- `npm run build:apply` builds the console, then this site into dist-apply/landing/. `scripts/deploy-apply.sh` copies all of dist-apply and functions/, so nothing extra is needed.
- `functions/_middleware.ts` lets every `/landing` path and `/robots.txt` through without a login.
- `functions/landing/[[path]].ts` (apply site only): redirects /landing and /landing/workflow to their trailing-slash URLs, serves built pages and files, and answers anything else under /landing with the landing 404 (noindex) instead of the console shell. On application.atriveo.com it passes straight through to that site's own public/landing/.
- `apply-public/robots.txt` disallows the console and allows /landing/; the sitemap is /landing/sitemap.xml.
